Navy is the single accent — used for the logo, dividers, buttons, and marks. It never competes with a second color; contrast comes from ink, weight, and space instead.

Page background — the one deliberate gradient

--gradient-page · paper → cream diagonal wash, with a faint navy bleed from the top-right corner. Every other surface in the system (buttons, cards, pills, dividers) stays flat — this is the single exception to the "no gradients" rule, reserved for the body background only.

06 — Layout

Grid & spacing

  • Asymmetric editorial grid — sections avoid centered, boxed layouts in favor of offset columns.
  • Max content width 1200px, generous outer gutter that scales with viewport.
  • No drop shadows, no gradients. Cards and inputs flat (0 radius); buttons and tags full pill — precise and drafted, not soft.
