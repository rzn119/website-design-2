Studio

How we work

Adjectif Creation is a multidisciplinary design and research studio. We work across spatial design, product design, and an integrated shop for the studio's own objects — for brands and individuals who use physical space to sell what they make.

Approach

Every brief starts as a question, not a form. We treat a retail interior and a table with the same rigor: what does this object need to say, and how little can it say it with? The work that comes out the other side reads as drafted rather than decorated — precise lines, deliberate materials, nothing left to explain itself twice.

We keep research and production close together. The same team that measures a site or tests a material also draws the final line — nothing is handed off far enough away to lose the reason it was drawn that way.

Process

From question to object

01 — Research

We start on site or in the studio with the brand, the material, and the constraint — mapping what the space or object actually needs to do before drawing anything.

02 — Draft

Ideas get tested as drawings first: plans, elevations, line studies. Nothing goes to production until the drawing can explain itself without us in the room.

03 — Build

We stay close through fabrication and install, on pop-ups and products alike, so the built thing matches the drawing — not a simplified version of it.

Disciplines

Three ways we work

  • Spatial design — pop-ups and retail environments for brands that use physical space to market what they make.
  • Product design — furniture and digital objects, drawn with the same rigor as a room.
  • Studio shop — objects we design, make, and sell directly, in small runs.
