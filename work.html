Maison Rou

A pop-up retail experience, Paris

Six weeks, one corner site off Rue de Charonne, and a brief to make a temporary space feel permanent. We built the whole interior around a single canvas canopy and a kit of unfinished oak fixtures that could go up and come down in a day — nothing bolted, nothing that couldn't be reused on the next site. The result reads as considered rather than provisional: a pop-up that doesn't announce itself as one.
