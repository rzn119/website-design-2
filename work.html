Selected Work

Designing for character, mood, texture, and intelligence that turns projects into memorable experiences.

Pop-ups, retail interiors, furniture, and digital objects — every project below started as a question about what a space or object needed to do.

01

Soilware

a circular dining museum of cutlery
Location — Yarra Valley, Victoria, Australia Year — 2025

The project seeks to respond to the client's demand to build a future museum that showcases and educate the public about its Red Dot award winning product '2050 Edible Cutlery'. We more often associate culinary practice with the food, but this project explores another essential component — cutlery — and attempts to embed it into the agricultural ecosystem. A restaurant is often the best mediator with the public, this project is beyond a conventional dining space as it integrates the life cycle of the cutlery to also be part of the dining experience.

02

Interior renovation for old house

transforming a 20-yr old house into contemporary style
Location — Doncaster, Melbourne, Australia Year — 2026

A single flagship space designed to carry the brand's full range without a single point-of-sale fixture in sight — circulation, lighting, and a wayfinding system built from the same material palette as the product itself. We treated the signage as furniture: cut from the same materials, finished the same way, meant to be lived with rather than noticed. The store reads like a considered room, not a retail set.
