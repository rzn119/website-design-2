Adjectif Creation is a multidisciplinary design and research studio working across spatial design, product design, and furniture — for people and brands who want to be felt, not just seen.

STUDIO

Designs that redefine.

Our practice moves across scales without treating any one of them as the "real" work — a storefront, a chair, and a wellness assessment all start from the same question: what does this need to say, and how little can it say it with.

Alongside client work, we design and produce a small collection of our own objects, sold directly through the studio — a way of keeping our hands in material and finish even between commissions.

01

Spatial Design

Retail interiors, pop-ups, and exhibition environments built around how people actually move through a space.

02

Product Design

Furniture and objects designed for production — ours and our clients' — from first sketch to finished piece.

03

Wellness Analysis

Research into how a space affects the people using it, feeding directly back into the design decisions we make.
